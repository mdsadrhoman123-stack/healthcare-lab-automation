# 02 · The client journey

What this looks like from the outside, for **Longevity / hormone-optimization clinic (US)**.

---

### 01 — A result comes back

Ingestion validates it before anything is stored.

### 02 — It is stored safely

Encrypted at rest, and the access is logged — because in healthcare who looked matters as much as what was stored.

### 03 — It is read against the right ranges

Longevity and hormone protocols do not use standard clinical ranges. The interpretation uses the practice's own.

### 04 — A physician decides the edge cases

Anything outside expected parameters goes to a physician queue. The system assists; it never decides.

### 05 — The patient hears clearly

A summary with clinical context, delivered securely.

### 06 — And it is all auditable

Every touch of patient data can be reconstructed afterwards.

---

## The one decision that shaped everything else

Ingestion, encrypted storage with access logging, interpretation against the practice's own reference ranges, physician review routing for anything outside expected parameters, and secure patient communication — each stage designed against HIPAA's technical safeguards.
